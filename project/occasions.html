Chapter IV

Dressing for Occasions

Context is everything. The right cloth at the wrong event is still a mistake.

Office

Daily Professional

Wear: Navy or charcoal suit, white shirt, silk tie, oxford shoes.

Fabric: 9–11 oz wool. Breathable enough for indoor climate control.

Avoid: Loud patterns, open collars in conservative offices, sneakers.

Interview

First Impressions

Wear: Dark navy suit, white shirt, conservative tie, black oxfords.

Fabric: Smooth worsted wool — looks sharp and photographs well.

Avoid: Double breasted, bright colours, excessive accessories.

Business Dinner

Evening Professional

Wear: Dark suit, white or pale blue shirt, pocket square, leather shoes.

Fabric: Fine wool or wool-mohair blend for a subtle sheen.

Avoid: Brown shoes with charcoal (debated, but navy is safer), polo shirts.

Summer Event

Warm Weather Formal

Wear: Light grey or beige linen suit, white shirt, loafers, no tie.

Fabric: Linen or tropical wool (6–8 oz). Unlined jacket preferred.

Avoid: Heavy flannel, dark colours that absorb heat, polyester blends.

Presentation

Public Speaking

Wear: Mid-grey or navy suit, crisp white shirt, minimal pattern tie.

Fabric: Matte wool — avoids shine under stage lighting.

Avoid: Busy patterns on camera, shiny silk ties, distracting jewellery.

Smart Casual Friday

Relaxed Professional

Wear: Blazer, chinos, oxford shirt, loafers or clean white sneakers.

Fabric: Cotton chinos, wool blazer, cotton or linen shirt.

Avoid: Jeans with holes, graphic tees, athletic wear.

Quick Decision Guide

Question If Yes If No
Is it after 6 PM? Darker colours, finer fabrics Lighter tones acceptable
Will you meet senior leadership? Full suit, conservative tie Blazer and trousers may suffice
Is the venue outdoors in summer? Linen or cotton, no tie Standard wool suit
Is it a first meeting? Dress one level above expected Match the environment

"Being overdressed is easier to recover from than being underdressed."

— A tailor's wisdom
