Chapter V

Building Your Wardrobe

Fewer pieces, better made. A capsule wardrobe is the essence of quiet luxury.

The Capsule Principle

A professional capsule wardrobe needs no more than 15–20 core pieces that mix and match across seasons. Quality over quantity — one canvassed suit outlasts three fused ones.

Build in this order: suits first, then shirts, then shoes, then accessories. Each layer depends on the one beneath it.

Buy less.
Choose well.
Make it last.

  • Wool overcoat for winter
  • Linen suit for summer events

Garment Care

Fine clothing rewards proper care. Neglect is the enemy of quiet luxury.

Suits & Jackets

Brush after each wear with a clothes brush. Dry clean only 1–2 times per year. Hang on wide cedar hangers. Rest 48 hours between wears.

Shirts

Wash cotton shirts after 1–2 wears. Iron while slightly damp for crisp collars. Store folded or on hangers with proper collar stays inserted.

Shoes

Use shoe trees after every wear. Polish leather weekly. Rotate between two pairs to allow leather to dry and recover its shape.

Storage Rules

  • Never store wool in plastic — breathable garment bags only
  • Cedar blocks deter moths better than chemical repellents
  • Fold knitwear; never hang cashmere or wool sweaters
  • Keep shoes away from direct sunlight to prevent leather cracking
  • Audit your wardrobe each season — donate what no longer fits or wears
