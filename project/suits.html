Suits & Jackets

The suit is the uniform of professionalism — when cut and coloured with restraint.

The Foundation Three

Before expanding, master these three colours. They cover 90% of professional life.

Navy

The most versatile suit colour. Works morning to evening, pairs with every shirt and tie combination. Mid-navy (not too dark, not electric) is the quiet luxury standard.

Charcoal Grey

Slightly more formal than navy. Ideal for presentations, negotiations, and winter months. Medium charcoal flatters most complexions.

Mid-Grey

The understated choice for spring and summer business. Lighter than charcoal but still authoritative. Pair with white or light blue shirts.

Single vs. Double Breasted

Single breasted, two-button is the universal business standard. It is versatile, easy to layer, and appropriate in nearly every professional setting.

Double breasted adds presence and formality. Best reserved for confident dressers and occasions where you want to command the room — not for a first interview.

  • Single breasted: daily office, client meetings, travel
  • Double breasted: formal events, winter, senior leadership
  • Blazers: separate jackets for smart casual layering

Business Formal vs. Smart Professional

Element Business Formal Smart Professional
Suit Matching wool suit, dark colour Suit or blazer with contrasting trousers
Shirt White or light blue, cotton White, blue, or subtle stripe
Tie Silk, solid or subtle pattern Optional — open collar acceptable
Shoes Oxford or derby, polished leather Loafers or clean derbies
Accessories Conservative watch, leather belt Pocket square, minimal jewellery

Jacket Styles

Beyond the suit — sport coats and blazers for layered professionalism.

Sport Coat

Textured & Relaxed

Tweed, herringbone, or hopsack in earthy tones. Worn with flannel or wool trousers in a different colour. The cornerstone of smart casual elegance.

Blazer

Crisp & Naval

Navy wool with gold or mother-of-pearl buttons. Originally from naval tradition. Pairs with grey flannel trousers or chinos for a timeless look.
